Privacy policy

Applies to the public beta (0.2.0-beta.1).

The short version

Token Sidekick runs on your computer. The beta has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and it doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.

What the app reads, locally

  • Claude Code: when you click Connect, it adds a status line and hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json (after making a backup). These give it your usage percentages and reset times, plus event names such as "a tool was used" and the tool's name. It never reads your prompts, Claude's replies or your files.
  • Codex CLI: it reads the rate limits and activity events from Codex's own log files in ~/.codex/sessions. It only reads them and never changes Codex's settings.
  • Your data (todos, habits, items and Workshop creations) stays in ~/.claude-sidekick on your PC. A small log file there helps with bug reports and is only shared if you attach it yourself.

What goes over the network

  • Update checks to GitHub (github.com), which sees your IP address like any website. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links you click, such as "Report a bug", open in your browser. Bug reports are public GitHub issues and include only what you see before posting.

Later features

If we add optional accounts (the community gallery, or purchases), this page will be updated before they go live, including what is stored and how to delete it.
